    About this data & this site

    Cleaned NCAA women's college basketball rosters, a stable cross-season player ID, and the aggregates built on them.

    What this is

    The repository behind this site holds one cleaned roster CSV per season (2020-21 onward) plus a cross-season player identifier, wbb_id, that follows a player across teams and seasons. The original roster information is scraped from team websites and augmented by individual research and editing; team information comes from the NCAA.

    This data is a project of the JOUR479X class, Sports Data Analysis & Visualization, at the Philip Merrill College of Journalism at the University of Maryland. Please credit the Sports Roster Data project at the University of Maryland.

    Student contributors: Ben Baruch, Michael Charles, Shane Connuck, Ian Decker, Jetson Ku, Tanner Malinowski, Varun Shankar, Rina Torchinsky and Dylan Manfre. This project was supervised and edited by Derek Willis, lecturer in data and computational journalism.

    Download the data

      All files live in the repository, are rebuilt by the cleaning pipeline, and carry one row per player per season. The roster explorer can also export any filtered view as CSV. This site is rebuilt automatically whenever the underlying data changes.

      Data dictionary

      The site's roster explorer and downloads use these columns:

      wbb_id
      Stable cross-season player identifier (wbb-######). The same player keeps her ID across seasons and teams; built in player_ids.Rmd.
      ncaa_id
      The team's NCAA id — the stable team key. Team names change over the years; ids don't.
      team
      Team name, standardized against teams.csv.
      name
      Player name as listed on the team's roster.
      jersey
      Jersey number, when the roster lists one.
      year_clean
      Standardized class year (Freshman … Sixth Year). Rosters may mean either academic year or athletic eligibility by this field, and COVID eligibility rules added extra years — read it as the roster presented it.
      redshirt
      1 when the roster marks the player as a redshirt.
      position_clean
      Standardized position; may be a combination such as GUARD/FORWARD.
      primary_position
      GUARD, FORWARD, CENTER or WING, derived from the position field.
      total_inches
      Height in total inches, so comparisons are possible. Roster values outside 58–90 inches are treated as parse errors and left blank.
      hometown_clean
      Hometown as printed on the roster, uppercased. Parsed, not normalized — spelling variants remain.
      state_clean
      US state or territory abbreviation; blank for hometowns outside the US.
      country_clean
      Country, standardized against a FIBA-derived list; USA for US hometowns.
      hs_clean
      Standardized high school, where one was matched (~44% of US player-seasons; see HIGH_SCHOOL_STANDARDIZATION.md).
      previous_school_clean
      Previous school as listed by the roster — sparse, and the least consistent field in the data.
      conference
      The team's conference, from teams.csv.
      division
      NCAA division (I, II, III), plus NAIA where identified.
      url
      The roster page this row was taken from.

      Player pages add:

      match confidence
      How the cross-season ID was assigned: high or medium confidence for matched players, or a single-season record when the player appears in only one season's data.
      seasons
      The player's season-by-season timeline — season, team, class year, redshirt flag and roster URL.

      Known limitations

      • Transfer counts are a floor. Cross-season matching is automatic for clear cases; borderline matches wait for human adjudication (wbb_id_review_queue.csv in the repository) and are not yet counted as moves. Counts can only rise.
      • Season coverage runs through what wbb_rosters_combined.csv contains — currently 2020-21 through 2025-26. The newest season file appears here once the annual cross-season ID pipeline is re-run.
      • Hometowns are parsed but not normalized ("CHICAGO, ILL" can appear next to "CHICAGO, IL"); city tabulations on the geography page are best-effort.
      • Class years may reflect academic or athletic standing — the COVID eligibility freeze makes them noisier than usual.
      • Previous school is filled for only a minority of rows and its formatting varies.
      • Heights outside 58–90 inches are treated as scrape/parse errors and excluded from aggregates.
